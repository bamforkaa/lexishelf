Generate panlex_korean_fallback.db from the pinned, checksum-verified
PanLex 2019-09-01 CSV snapshot. See docs/dictionary-sources.md and
docs/public-dictionary-artifacts.md for the reviewed source, checksum and license
requirements, and docs/dictionary-packs.md for the local pack layout and build
command. The generated index contains only direct same-meaning relations for
explicitly reviewed PanLex language varieties.

The embedded LICENSE.txt in that exact 2019-09-01 artifact grants CC0 1.0
Universal. The current PanLex website publishes different license terms, so
do not assume that newer PanLex artifacts are CC0. Review every replacement
artifact independently. PanLex recommends citing https://panlex.org/ or its
2014 LREC paper.
