Kaikki/Wiktextract generated indexes are intentionally not committed.

The pinned source for this implementation is the official Kaikki raw JSONL
extraction based on the 2026-08-05 English Wiktionary dump and published on
2026-08-23. Its reviewed SHA-256 and source requirements are documented in
docs/dictionary-sources.md and docs/public-dictionary-artifacts.md. The local
pack layout and build command are documented in docs/dictionary-packs.md.

The extracted dictionary text remains English Wiktionary content under
CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL; the MIT license of the Wiktextract software does not
replace the data license. This app uses the CC BY-SA 4.0 option, retains
source provenance. Index schema v2 retains at most two source-ordered
contributor-authored usage examples (`type=example` without an external `ref`)
per sense. It excludes attributed quotations, example translations, audio,
images, raw categories, link graphs, and etymology text.
