The Korean Basic Dictionary index is intentionally not committed.

Generate korean_basic_dictionary.db from the official full JSON download. See
docs/dictionary-sources.md for the reviewed source and license requirements, and
docs/dictionary-packs.md for the local pack layout and build command. The generated
index contains text from the Korean Basic Dictionary provided by the National
Institute of Korean Language under CC BY-SA 2.0 KR. Audio, image, video, and
pronunciation media are excluded.
